# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Students at Westminster College (all campuses) — mostly on phones, opening the app in short bursts between classes to see what's blowing up, what's true, and what's funny. A smaller set of moderators (student council / staff) run the admin queue to keep posts from becoming a pile-on.

## Product Purpose

Glitched is the digital school square: a vertical feed of campus posts (text, images, covers), reactions and comments, plus a discover surface and a moderation queue. Posting is low-friction — no login wall (auth is intentionally stubbed open for now). Success means the feed feels alive and current, and anyone can drop a glitch in seconds.

## Brand Personality

Vibey, cheeky, streetwear. Gossip treated as culture, not news. Voice is dry and in-on-the-joke — like the friend who texts the group chat, not a school newsletter. Retro-arcade energy: pixel type, chromatic-aberration glitch, gold / crimson / pitch black. Local, never institutional — "Glitched" is the corrupted, the exposed, the tea that breaks the feed.

## Anti-references

- **Generic social app** (Instagram / TikTok clones): rounded card grids, like counts, influencer chrome, infinite-scroll sameness.
- **AI-default cream dashboard**: beige pages, muted grays, thin type, tasteful-but-anonymous sameness.
- **School portal / edu SaaS**: bootstrap blue, notices, attendance energy — institutional and joyless.
- Kids / teen cartoon app: bubble letters, emoji walls, candy gradients.

## Design Principles

1. **The square, not the feed** — surfaces should feel like a place people hang out, not a timeline machine.
2. **Merch, not UI** — brand moments read like something you'd wear: fat type, print offsets, hard color, no soft gradients.
3. **Fast to post, fast to read** — no ceremony between a student and the box.
4. **Moderation is first-class** — the admin queue is product, not an afterthought.
5. **Dark, loud, legible** — pitch-black canvas; gold and crimson do the shouting; contrast never sacrificed for vibe.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Body text ≥ 4.5:1 and large text ≥ 3:1 on the pitch-black canvas; gold and paper carry the load, crimson is accent-only for small text.
- Every animation respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Feed, admin, and forms are keyboard-navigable with visible focus states.
- No auth wall yet — the whole surface must be usable without an account.
